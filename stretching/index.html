Stretching and flexibility guides

Flexibility improves with regular, patient practice rather than extreme stretches. These guides show how to warm up, which stretches matter most and how often to practise, from a simple 30-day plan to the front and middle splits.

Stretch to mild tension, never to pain, and breathe slowly. Warm muscles stretch further and with less risk, so do your stretching after a short warm-up or a workout.
