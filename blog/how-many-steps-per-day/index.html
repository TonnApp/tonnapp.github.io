How Many Steps a Day Do You Need, and How to Count Them

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Walking is the easiest exercise to fit into a day. A frequently quoted target is 10,000 steps, but that is a convenient round number, not a rule. Studies suggest that health benefits begin well below it, and more steps generally help up to a point.

Choosing your goal

  • Sedentary start: measure your current average over a week and add 1,000 steps.
  • General health: around 7,000–10,000 steps a day is a reasonable aim for many adults.
  • Weight loss or fitness: a higher number combined with exercise and food habits.

Ways to add steps

  • Walk 10 minutes after each meal.
  • Get off one stop earlier or park farther away.
  • Take phone calls while walking.
  • Use stairs instead of the lift.

Track steps and distance

A phone or a watch counts steps; a weekly total is more useful than any single day. Note the distance too if you run or hike, and look at your 14-day trend.

Listen to your body

Increase gradually, wear comfortable shoes and rest if you have joint or foot pain. If you have a health condition, ask your doctor about a safe level.
