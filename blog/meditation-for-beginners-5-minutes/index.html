Meditation for Beginners: How to Start in 5 Minutes

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Meditation is simply training attention. You do not need to empty the mind or sit for an hour. Five minutes a day is a good beginning, and the skill grows with regular practice.

How to begin

  1. Find a quiet spot and sit comfortably on a chair or cushion with a straight back.
  2. Set a timer for five minutes so you do not watch the clock.
  3. Close your eyes or soften your gaze.
  4. Follow the breath. Notice the air entering and leaving, or the rise and fall of the belly.
  5. When the mind wanders, and it will, calmly return to the breath. This returning is the practice, not a failure.

Common questions

  • Do I need to cross my legs? No. A chair is fine.
  • What if I fall asleep? Try a different time of day or sit more upright.
  • How soon will I feel something? Some people feel calmer at once; for others the effect appears after weeks. Practise without expectations.

Build the habit

  • Pick a fixed time, such as after waking.
  • Start with five minutes and add one minute a week.
  • Use a guide with a gentle bell if silence is hard.
