Interval Running for Beginners: Your First Month

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

If you have never run, or have not run for a long time, alternating running and walking is the safest way to start. It builds endurance without overloading joints and tendons.

Before you start

  • Wear running shoes that fit well.
  • Pick a flat, soft surface if possible.
  • Walk briskly for five minutes first.
  • Run at a pace where you can still speak in short sentences.

Four-week plan (three sessions a week)

  • Week 1: 1 minute running, 2 minutes walking, 8 times.
  • Week 2: 2 minutes running, 2 minutes walking, 6 times.
  • Week 3: 3 minutes running, 2 minutes walking, 5 times.
  • Week 4: 5 minutes running, 1–2 minutes walking, 4 times.

Rest or walk on the other days. If a week feels too hard, repeat it before moving on.

Support your running

  • Strengthen legs and core twice a week with squats, lunges and planks.
  • Stretch after every run.
  • Increase total time by no more than about 10% a week.

Warning signs

Sharp pain, pain that gets worse during a run or lasts for days are reasons to stop and rest. Seek a doctor’s advice if it persists.
