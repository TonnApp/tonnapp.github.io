Breathing Techniques for Calm: 4 Simple Exercises

Published: 2026-11-15 · 2 min read

Breathing is the one body function that works automatically but that we can also control. Slow, steady breathing signals to the nervous system that you are safe, which is why breathing exercises are used for relaxation, before sleep and during stressful moments. Here are four simple techniques you can try today.

The basics

  • Sit comfortably with a straight back or lie down.
  • Breathe through the nose unless a technique says otherwise.
  • Never strain. The breath should feel easy and quiet.
  • Five minutes is enough. Regular short sessions work better than one long one.

1. Belly (diaphragmatic) breathing

Place one hand on the chest and one on the belly. Inhale slowly through the nose so the belly rises more than the chest; exhale slowly and let the belly sink. Continue for three to five minutes. This is the foundation for all other techniques.

2. Box breathing

Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4, exhale for 4, hold for 4, then repeat. The even rhythm focuses attention and is popular for staying calm under pressure. If four counts feel long, start with three.

3. Longer exhale

Inhale for 4 counts and exhale for 6 or 8. A longer exhale tends to be relaxing, so this technique is a good choice when you feel tense or have trouble falling asleep.

4. The 4-7-8 breath

Inhale quietly through the nose for 4 counts, hold for 7, and exhale slowly through the mouth for 8. Start with just four cycles. Breath holds can feel strong, so do not push.

Tips for beginners

  • Practise at the same time every day, for example after waking or before bed.
  • If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, return to normal breathing.
  • Keep the shoulders and jaw relaxed.
  • If counting distracts you, use a visual or audio guide.
