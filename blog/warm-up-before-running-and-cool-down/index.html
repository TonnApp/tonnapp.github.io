Warm-Up Before Running and Cool-Down After: A Simple Routine

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

A good warm-up prepares your muscles, joints and heart for the effort, while a cool-down brings the body back to rest and helps flexibility. Together they take about ten minutes and can reduce the chance of discomfort.

Warm-up (5 minutes)

  1. Brisk walk (2 minutes) to raise your heart rate gradually.
  2. Leg swings (10 each leg) forward and sideways.
  3. Walking lunges or lunge steps (10–12) to open the hips.
  4. Ankle and hip circles for joint mobility.
  5. Light jog for the last minute.

Save long static stretches for after the run. Before it, choose dynamic movements.

Cool-down (5 minutes)

  1. Slow walk (2–3 minutes) until your breathing settles.
  2. Calf stretch 30 seconds each leg.
  3. Quad stretch 30 seconds each leg.
  4. Hamstring and hip stretches 30 seconds each.

Common mistakes

  • Starting at full speed with cold muscles.
  • Stopping dead after a hard run and sitting down at once.
  • Skipping the routine on short runs; that is when it is easiest to do.
