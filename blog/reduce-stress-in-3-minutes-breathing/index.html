How to Reduce Stress in 3 Minutes: Breathing at Work

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

When stress spikes in the middle of a workday, you do not need a quiet room or a mat. A three-minute breathing break at your desk can help you reset. Here is a simple routine.

The 3-minute reset

  1. Sit tall (30 seconds). Feet on the floor, shoulders relaxed, hands on the thighs.
  2. Slow the breath (2 minutes). Inhale through the nose for four counts, exhale through the nose or pursed lips for six. Keep it easy and quiet.
  3. Scan and release (30 seconds). Notice the jaw, shoulders and hands and let them soften.

A longer exhale tends to calm the body, which is why it is the focus of this routine.

Add a stretch

If you can stand, do a gentle forward fold for ten breaths, with the knees soft. It releases the neck and the back after sitting.

A seated twist also helps open the chest and back.

When to repeat

  • Before a meeting or a difficult conversation.
  • After a stressful email or call.
  • Every couple of hours as a habit.

If breathing exercises make you dizzy or anxious, return to normal breathing. The approach is a calming practice, not medical treatment.
