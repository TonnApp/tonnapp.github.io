Stretching After a Workout: What, How Long and Why

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

A short stretch after training helps the body calm down and keeps joints moving freely. It will not remove all soreness, but it is a pleasant cool-down and a good habit for flexibility. Stretch when the muscles are warm, right after your session.

The basic rules

  • Spend 5–10 minutes, focusing on the muscles you just used.
  • Hold each stretch for 20–40 seconds without bouncing.
  • Breathe slowly and relax into the stretch with each exhale.
  • Stretch to mild tension, never to pain.

A simple cool-down

Quad stretch. Standing, hold the foot behind you and keep the knees together. Use the wall for balance.

Calf stretch. Press the heel down with the leg straight behind you, hands on a wall.

Chest stretch. Open the arms behind the back to release the chest after pressing exercises.

Hamstrings and hips. A standing or seated forward fold and a figure-four stretch.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the cool-down because you are in a hurry. Even three minutes help.
  • Holding the breath.
  • Long static stretches before a heavy or explosive workout; do dynamic movements instead.
