Yoga for Your Back: Gentle Exercises for Desk Workers

Published: 2026-11-15 · 1 min read

Long hours at a desk tighten the hips, round the shoulders and leave the lower back stiff. Gentle yoga can restore movement and relieve everyday tension. This article covers common stiffness, not injuries or serious pain: if you have acute back pain, numbness or a diagnosed condition, talk to a doctor before exercising.

Gentle poses to try

Cat-Cow. Moves the whole spine. Do 8–10 slow rounds with your breath.

Child’s Pose. Lengthens the back and rests the muscles. Stay for 5–8 breaths.

Low Cobra. A small, gentle backbend that wakes up the back muscles. Keep the elbows close and the neck long.

Knees to Chest. Lying on your back, hug the knees and rock gently to release the lower back.

Reclining twist. A soft rotation for the spine and the sides of the body.

How to practise

  • Do a short sequence every day, ideally in the evening or during a break.
  • Move slowly and stay within a comfortable range.
  • Stand up and move every 45–60 minutes of sitting; no pose can cancel a full day without movement.

What to avoid

  • Deep forward folds with straight legs if the lower back is sensitive; bend the knees instead.
  • Forcing twists or deep backbends.
  • Any movement that causes sharp pain, tingling or numbness in the legs.
