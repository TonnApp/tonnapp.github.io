Yoga guides

Yoga is a practice of movement, breathing and attention. It can be as gentle as a ten-minute evening stretch or as demanding as a long strength-based flow. This page collects our practical guides so you can find the right starting point and follow a clear path.

Not sure where to begin? Start with the beginner plan, add a short morning or evening routine, and use the breathing guides to relax. Always move within a comfortable range and stop if something hurts.
