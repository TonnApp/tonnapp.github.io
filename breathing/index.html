Breathing and meditation guides

Slow, steady breathing signals to the nervous system that you are safe, which is why breathing exercises are popular for relaxation, before sleep and during stressful moments. These guides give you simple techniques you can try in a few minutes, plus a gentle introduction to meditation.

Never strain: the breath should feel easy and quiet. If an exercise makes you dizzy or uncomfortable, return to normal breathing. These practices support relaxation and are not medical treatment.
