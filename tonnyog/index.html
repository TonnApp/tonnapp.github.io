Frequently asked questions

Is TonnYog free?

You can start for free with the Simple level. Extra levels (Standard and Advanced) are one-time in-app purchases.

Do I need internet to do yoga with TonnYog?

No. The app works fully offline once it is installed.

Is TonnYog good for beginners?

Yes. It has beginner-friendly 30-day programs, a trial practice that teaches the interface, and a pose guide with step-by-step instructions.

Which platforms are supported?

TonnYog is available for Android on Google Play.

Can I create my own yoga routine?

Yes. The routine builder lets you choose poses, their order and duration, then save the program and practise it any time.
